02 / Methodology

A Structured, Hypothesis-Driven Approach

Design is not decoration; it is the deliberate reduction of user friction. My 4-step framework ensures every design decision is anchored in evidence, validated through rapid testing, and engineered with precision.

01

Discover & Empathize

Immersing in the user's world through qualitative contextual inquiry, diary studies, and quantitative analytics to expose true pain points.

Key Artifacts
User Interviews Heuristic Audit Competitive Matrix
02

Define & Architect

Synthesizing research into razor-sharp problem definitions, Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD), user flows, and hierarchical information architecture.

Key Artifacts
Information Architecture Persona Archetypes User Journey Maps
03

Prototype & Validate

Exploring low-fidelity wireframes followed by high-fidelity interactive prototypes. Running usability sprints to test hypotheses before engineering.

Key Artifacts
Interactive ProtoPie Usability Testing (Maze) Micro-Interactions
04

Refine & Ship

Transforming validated screens into production-ready design tokens, accessible components, pairing closely with engineering for pixel-perfect launch.

Key Artifacts
Design Tokens (W3C) Storybook Specs WCAG AAA Audits

04 / Background

Designing at the Intersection of Computer Applications and Human Empathy

As a 1st-year Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) student and passionate UI/UX designer, I work at the intersection of computer science, interaction architecture, and visual aesthetics. While studying data structures, programming paradigms, and software principles, I channel that technical rigor into building intuitive, accessible digital interfaces that solve real user problems.

I believe the most impactful designers understand the technical reality of the web. Because I write semantic HTML, modern CSS, and JavaScript alongside my BCA curriculum, I design with direct empathy for developers—ensuring design tokens, edge cases, and WCAG accessibility standards are built into the foundation from day one.
